# Moneta Overview

The Overview page is now a real authenticated financial snapshot.

It shows:
- User identity
- Income and expenses
- Net worth
- Savings and investments
- Debt
- Risk tolerance
- Emergency runway
- Financial health score
- Goals
- Debt snapshot
- Optimiser allocation
- Current priorities
- Account connection status

The Home page's "Explore your finances" button goes to login.html.
After login, users reach overview.html.
The Overview sidebar item also loads overview.html.
